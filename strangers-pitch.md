# Strangers

**New ideas for strange times. Proven while others are still planning.**

---

Marketing organizations move too slowly — to adopt new technology, to shift strategy, to respond to market change. The consulting industry can't fix this because it's built the same way: staff generalists against briefs, deliver recommendations, move on. Optimized to capture revenue. Predictable thinking on someone else's timeline.

Strangers is built upside down.

We don't start from a brief. We start from conviction — a living ontology of the ideas that matter most right now, tracked in real time, backed by proprietary data. We show up with a point of view before the client asks for one.

We deploy that conviction through three layers:

**Strong opinions.** Named theses on where value is moving — researched, tracked, pressure-tested. Not thought leadership. Inventory. Where traditional firms sell time, we sell access to thinking that doesn't exist anywhere else.

**Forward-deployed consultants.** Senior strategists embedded in your decision-making rhythm. No junior teams. No back rooms. No big reveals. Continuous output you can react to, redirect, and build on — starting week one.

**Strategy as code.** We don't hand you a deck and wish you luck. We prototype the implication — a model, a tool, a working system. You don't have to imagine what the strategy means. You can see it, test it, and build on it. Days, not months.

The whole system compounds. Every engagement makes the ontology sharper, every prototype becomes a reusable pattern, every client interaction feeds structured data back into the intelligence layer. The firm gets more valuable with each engagement — not just larger.

We're not an AI firm. The technology is invisible. We're not a discount model. We price on the value of the outcome. We're building in the gap between McKinsey and Droga5 — where companies need new thinking *and* proof it works, from people senior enough to be trusted with both.

**Strangers.** We already know what matters. So when the conversation starts, we're not discovering — we're deploying.
